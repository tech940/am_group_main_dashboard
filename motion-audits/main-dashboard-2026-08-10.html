MOTION AUDIT · DESIGN-MOTION-PRINCIPLES

MD Approvals — a money queue that is rightly quiet, with three loud silences

The MD Approvals section aggregates purchase orders, petty cash and vendor payments into one executive work queue. Motion today is minimal by design: 150ms color transitions, spinners during requests, a 200ms dialog — and nothing else.

app/md-approvals/md-approvals-client.tsx (1,247 lines) · Next.js 16 + Tailwind + Radix Dialog · audited 2026-08-10

6 findings 1 critical 2 important 3 opportunities

OVERALL

This is one of the most restrained screens an audit of this kind will see — no pulse rings, no stagger spam, no hover-scale carpets, and the highest-frequency action (approve) is correctly instant. The problems are the opposite kind: three UI surfaces that should move don't. The bulk action bar and the outcome panels snap into the document flow and shove the table around with zero transition, and the OS reduced-motion setting is only honored by KIA-branded animations, not by this section's dialog or spinners. Fix the three silences and this screen is genuinely done.

ISSUES TO ADDRESS

CriticalEmilAccessibility

Reduced-motion is only honored by KIA-branded animations

WhatThe global @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) block in globals.css:1992 targets only .kia-skeleton, .kia-anim-* and [data-kia-motion]. This section's motion — the Radix dialog's zoom+slide enter, three animate-spin loops, and every transition — plays at full strength for users who asked the OS to reduce motion.

Why it mattersAccessibility handling is the one non-negotiable in this audit's rulebook, and the current guard gives a false sense of coverage: it exists, it just doesn't apply to most of the app. The dialog's zoom+slide is exactly the class of motion the preference exists to suppress.

Recommended motionWiden the global guard (or use Tailwind's motion-reduce: variants) so dialogs collapse to a plain 150ms fade and decorative transitions drop to 0.001ms under reduce. Spinners may keep rotating — progress indication is functional — but zoom, slide and hover polish should flatten.

ImportantJakubMotion gap

The bulk action bar snaps into flow and shoves the table

What{selected.length > 0 && …} inserts a ~72px sticky card into the document the instant the first row is clicked — no enter, no exit, and every row below jumps down under the cursor mid-selection.

Why it mattersThe MD is usually mid-click-run when this appears. Content shifting under the pointer between clicks is how the wrong row gets selected — on this screen, that's the wrong payment approved. The jump also reads as a glitch, not a feature arriving.

Recommended motionFast fade + 8px rise on the bar (160ms, ease-out custom curve), and animate the container's height so rows below settle rather than teleport. Exit mirrors at 120ms. Under reduced motion: instant, as today.

ImportantJakubMotion gap

Success and failure panels appear with no transition

WhatThe outcome panels — including the persistent partial-failure panel this screen is rightly proud of — pop into the flow above the table with zero motion, shifting everything below. Dismissing them snaps the layout back.

Why it mattersThe failure panel is the single most important element on the page when it shows. An abrupt appearance is easy to attribute to "the page glitched" rather than "something needs your attention" — the opposite of what a money-failure surface wants. A brief enter says this arrived on purpose.

Recommended motion180ms fade + 6px rise on enter, no exit animation needed on dismiss beyond a 120ms fade. Same reduce-motion collapse as everywhere else.
