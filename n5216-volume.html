Reading these numbers

  • The two matrices will not agree, and should not. 23,282 records carry a confirm date and 23,257 carry a delivery date, but they fall in different months. Compare a year total against the same year total, never a confirm month against a delivery month.
  • 72 records carry neither date and therefore appear in no cell of either matrix. They are in the 23,354 headline but not in the 23,282 or 23,257.
  • Delivery dates start in 2000, eight years before the first confirm. Five records sit in 2000, 2001, 2004, 2005 and 2006 — one per year, with no confirm date nearby. These are almost certainly keying errors rather than real deliveries.
  • Delivery runs seven days past today. The latest delivery date is 2026-08-29 against a latest confirm of 2026-08-20 — scheduled handovers already entered. August will keep growing.
  • 2026 is a partial year — January to August only. Do not compare it to a full year without pro-rating.
  • Duplicate VINs are negligible here. Deduplicating by VIN moves the yearly totals by 0–3 records, so the counts above are read as rows. That is not true of the branch codes.

Why the dashboard will not show you these numbers

Selecting N5216 in the Sales Report returns 126 records, not 23,354. The dealer filter resolves COALESCE(dealer_code_2, source_dealer_code, dealer_code, main_dealer_code), and dealer_code_2 holds N5203 on 23,207 historical rows — a code that appears in no other column and in no dealer dropdown.

So all Hyundai sales history before July 2026 is attributed to a dealer nobody can select. The counts on this page come from dealer_code directly, which is what the 99.4% figure refers to.
